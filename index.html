Welcome to

Possibility Management New Zealand

Possibility Management empowers you to change directions and create a regenerative way of life. With new thoughtware and a new way of living you can create completely new results not dependent on the circumstances. It works by converting inner barriers into fertilizer, doorways, rocket ships, or avoidable mud puddles along your path of evolution.

Possibility Management is upgraded thoughtware for catalysing change in the physical, intellectual, emotional or energetic obstacles blocking you and your team from using non-obvious possibilities.

For more than 40 years the originators of Possibility Management have passionately shared open-code tools and distinctions that bring people into greater coherence.

Today a worldwide community of trainers works tirelessly and highly inspired together to implement the shared vision of a global shift: build bridges between modern culture which brings humanity to its limits, and the next cultures where archetypal initiated men and women collaborate: Archiarchy.

To jump into this body of transformational work, you can visit the 700+ webpage directory of open-code resources, thought maps, and try-at-home experiments. If you're wanting to delve deeper into harnessing the powerful tools and distinctions discovered and implimented into the Possibility Management universe, there are books packed with practical thoughtware upgrades and to there are opportunities to dive in to supportive team spaces by joining a Possibility Team, Rage Club or experiencing the core training of Possibility Management, Expand the Box. Local upcoming trainings are listed towards the end of this page. Additionally, trainings, workshops, worktalks, and other spaces contexted in Possibility Management from around the globe can be found on the Noticeboard.

The core training

EXPAND THE BOX

Did you ever try to change something in your life?

The human mind is able to imagine a perfect result but the actual change doesn't happen in the mind. Outer change starts with inner change.

Supporting each other

There is a life before the Expand The Box Training and one after that. Probably you learned how to survive in conditions that no longer apply. The standard thinking and behavior patterns you adopted from your parents, your culture, and your education system could severely limit both the quality of your relationships and your ability to respond creatively.

POSSIBILITY LAB

Bring more consciousness in every day life

The purpose of a Possibility Lab is to create doorways and internal capacity to bring more consciousness in every day life. Healing and initiatory processes into adulthood.

Initiatory spaces

Possibility Labs are spaces of growth, connection, initiation and high level fun! Creating Next Culture. Together. Each participant is met where they are and goes through particular processes most valuable for them.

We come together as a village

We set up and take down the training spaces together, prepare meals, and clean-up together. This builds the practical capacity to create a learning community: ongoing collaboration.
